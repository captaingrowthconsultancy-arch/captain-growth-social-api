Privacy Policy

Captain Growth Social Manager
Last updated: 24 September 2026

Approval-ready draft for publication. Captain Growth Consultancy should review this policy before making it public.

1. Who operates the application

Captain Growth Social Manager is operated by Captain Growth Consultancy, Hazmieh, Lebanon. Contact: captaingrowth.consultancy@gmail.com or +961 70 737 381.

2. Scope

This is an internal application used to connect and manage Captain Growth Consultancy's own professional Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered as a public consumer service and does not permit unrelated users to create accounts.

3. Data processed

When an authorized operator connects a platform account, the application may process account identifiers, display name, username, avatar URL, public profile information, public video or media metadata, follower and engagement statistics, granted permission scopes, and OAuth access or refresh tokens. For approved publishing actions, it may process the media file, caption, and publishing settings supplied by the operator.

The application does not request or store social-platform passwords, payment-card data, or two-factor authentication codes.

4. Purposes

Data is used only to verify the connected account, retrieve authorized analytics, prepare and publish explicitly approved content, maintain the connection, diagnose errors, and protect the application from unauthorized access.

5. Legal basis and authorization

Processing is based on the authorized account operator's consent and the legitimate operational interests of Captain Growth Consultancy in managing its own business accounts. Access is limited to permissions granted through the relevant platform's official authorization flow.

6. Sharing

Captain Growth Consultancy does not sell connected-account data. Data is shared only with Meta, TikTok, hosting or infrastructure providers needed to operate the application, or authorities when legally required. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Storage and security

Application secrets and OAuth tokens are stored encrypted for the authorized Windows user using Windows Data Protection API. The application is bound to the local computer unless a separate public callback page is used. Reasonable access controls, least-privilege permissions, OAuth state validation, and redacted logs are used to reduce risk.

8. Retention and deletion

Connection data is retained only while the integration is active or as needed to meet legal and security obligations. An authorized operator may revoke access in the relevant platform settings and request deletion by contacting the address below. Local tokens and connection metadata will be deleted after a verified request and operational completion of the disconnection.

9. Rights and choices

Authorized operators may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data, subject to applicable law. Platform permissions can also be revoked directly through Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok account settings.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's capabilities or legal requirements change. The latest version will show its effective date.
